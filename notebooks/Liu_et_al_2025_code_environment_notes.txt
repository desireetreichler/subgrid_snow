To reproduce the environment used for the revisions of the Liu et al. 2025 paper, use the adjusted environment here without geoutils and xdem. 

Then, add geoutils, xdem, xsnow - but caution, old versions are required!  (local clones in the snowdepth project folder)     
- geoutils==0.0.10
- xdem==0.0.7
- xsnow - the version of liuh886/subgrid_snow:main. 

Possibly, also netcdf5 and h5netcdf need to be added to the environment.

For the calibration in one of the original notebooks, rioxarray is needed -> but this caused incompatibilities, thus there is an updated notebook that runs without this library.
